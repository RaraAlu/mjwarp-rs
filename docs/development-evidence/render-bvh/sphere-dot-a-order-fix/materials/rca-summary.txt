# sphere-dot-a-order-fix — RCA summary (evidence copy; full RCA lives in target/sphere-depth-rca.md, not committed)

## Root cause
U4 `render_sdf_reference` sphere-outline depth mismatch (cam0 215/76,800, max |ULP| 191; cam1 4/43,200,
max |ULP| 25; first pixel (230,69) seg=(2,5)) is the f32 **FMA contraction order of `a = dot(vec,vec)`
in the sphere analytic quadratic**, not a model/parity problem.

- reference (warp 1.15 GPU code generated for `ray.py::ray_sphere`/`_ray_quad`): `a` = mul(y*y) -> fma(x*x) -> fma(z*z)
- product before fix (`render_math.cu::dot3_ch0`): `a` = mul(x*x) -> fma(y*y) -> fma(z*z)
- `b`, `dd`, fused `det`, `1/a`, root selection and `depth = dist * -local_dir_z` are identical on both sides.

## How it was established (read-only, offline, zero GPU)
- **Product chain reproduces itself**: for all 44 diagnosed pixels (attempt2 read-only diagnostics),
  product camera matrix (f32 quat->mat chain) + world transform `fma(m2,z,fma(m0,x,mul(m1,y)))` + fused det
  + `a=dot3_ch0`, `b=dd=dot3_ba` reproduces the product depth **bit-exactly 44/44**.
- **Instruction-order enumeration**: holding that chain, each of the three dot products has 6 f32
  contraction orders (first term as a plain mul, then two fma steps) -> 6^3 = 216 combinations, times
  2 det formulations (fused / non-fused) = **432 variants**. Exactly the family with `a=(1,0,2)` and
  `b=(1,0,2)` reproduces the reference **44/44**; the `dd` order is irrelevant, so this shows up as
  **2 tied winners** (`dd=(0,1,2)` and `dd=(1,0,2)`) in the raw ranking. The product's variant
  (`a=(0,1,2)`) reproduces the reference **0/44**; every other variant is <= 37/44.
- **Mechanism / amplification**: `a` depends only on the ray direction; the two orders round to the same f32
  for most directions. At the sphere silhouette `det = b*b - a*c ~ 0`, so a 1-ULP difference in `a`
  is amplified by `sqrt(det)` into 7-191 ULP of depth. Sensitivity check: 1-ULP change of a single
  matrix column-2 element or of `dir[2]` shifts depth by 32-80 ULP at those pixels.
- **3.12 vs 3.15 parity is NOT the cause**: `cam_pos`, `cam_quat`, `cam_fovy` f64 values are bit-identical
  between the 3.12 product dump and the 3.15 capture recipe; sphere `geom_pos`/`geom_size` are the same.
  The product's camera matrix (f32 quat chain) does differ from `f32(mjData.cam_xmat)` in 4 of 9 elements
  by 1 ULP (and one near-zero element), but feeding each of the three matrices through the world transform
  gives identical depth at all 44 pixels -> recorded as an observation, not load-bearing here.
- **Why S7b1/S8 passed**: the old 747-pixel probe on scene-a cam1 was a sample with no counter-example
  (`a` happened to round identically for those directions and no pixel hit `det ~ 0` with a differing `a`),
  hence the previous "a uses component 0 first" note in `render_math.cu` was wrong.
